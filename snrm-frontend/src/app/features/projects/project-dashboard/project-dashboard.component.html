Archive this project

Downloads the whole experiment as one .zip: every network as its full XML document, the product catalogue, the variant lineage, every disruption scenario with its events, and every completed simulation run with its resolved parameters, its seed, its metric results and its time series. Runs that failed, were cancelled or are still queued are left out - they carry no results to interpret.

Nothing here is changed or removed by archiving. Restore it - into a new project, never merged into an existing one - from the project list. The archive records the simulation engine that produced its runs, and a restore on a different engine says so rather than refusing.
